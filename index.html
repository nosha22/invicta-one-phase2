❓ How to Play

Walk the real Visma Porto office. Explore both Invicta-One phases, test the skills for real, and collect your two passport stamps.

🕹️ Move & interact

  • WASD / arrow keys to walk — or the on-screen D-pad on mobile.
  • Stand next to something interactive → a hint chip appears on the map.
  • Press E (or tap the chip / the E button on the D-pad) to open it. Esc closes it.

🏢 Three floors, one building

  • You start on the Ground Floor — that's Phase 1. Find 🪜 stairs to go up.
  • The badge under the title always shows which floor you're on.
  • A stairwell up on the Ground Floor leads to the Second Floor (Phase 2). A stairwell up there leads to the Top Floor (Phase 3) — go see for yourself what's up there.

🚀 Ground Floor — Phase 1

  • The Kessel Run, Architect, and Holocron rooms each hold one completed prompting trial.
  • Walk in and press E → "Read" opens the full write-up: the task, the result, and the reusable "go further" artifact.
  • The 🧘 Prompt Sensei lives on this floor too — offer him a rough prompt for a stage/score/coaching tip.

🤖 Second Floor — Phase 2

  • Visit the Operative Lounge first to pick your on-screen character (cosmetic only).
  • Each skill room (Archivist Café, Scribe Booths, Sentinel Boardroom, Inspector Nook) lets you equip that skill.
  • With a skill equipped, go to any 🖥 terminal and paste a messy input. Three ways to run it:
    • ⚡ Instant demo — a deterministic in-browser analysis, no setup, works offline.
    • 🌩 Live AI (shared) — runs the real SKILL.md on a shared free model via this site's worker.
    • 🔑 Your Claude key — the most faithful option: add your own key (🔑 button, sidebar) and it calls Claude directly from your browser.

🎫 Passport stamps

Read all 3 Phase 1 trials to earn the Phase 1 stamp; equip all 4 skills to earn the Phase 2 stamp. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can finish later.

📊 Proof, not just claims

The Eval Log button shows the official determinism results, plus a live table of whatever you just ran in this tab — run the same skill twice and watch it check for a decision match.

🔒 One more thing

Curious what's upstairs from Phase 2? Take those stairs too — that's Phase 3, "The Autonomous Frontier." It hasn't been released yet, but the floor's already there, sealed.
